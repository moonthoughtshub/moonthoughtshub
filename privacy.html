Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Overview

MoonThoughts Hub ("we", "us", or "our") operates the website at moonthoughtshub.com and associated social media accounts. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information in connection with our website and automated content publishing system.

We are committed to protecting your privacy. This site does not require you to create an account, and we do not collect personal information from visitors beyond what is described below.

Information we collect

Website visitors: This website does not use cookies, tracking pixels, or analytics scripts. We do not collect names, email addresses, or any personally identifiable information from visitors. If you turn on the background music (the moon button), your browser remembers that choice on your own device; nothing is sent to us.

Social media: When you interact with our content on Instagram or Pinterest (likes, comments, saves, follows), that interaction is governed by that platform's privacy policy. We do not receive your personal data from those interactions beyond the aggregate metrics the platform makes available to content creators.

Third-party services: Our automated posting system uses the following third-party services:

  • Meta (Instagram Graph API) — used to publish content to our Instagram Business account. See Meta's Privacy Policy.
  • Pinterest API — used to publish our own images to our own Pinterest boards. See Pinterest's Privacy Policy and the Pinterest section below.
  • Cloudinary — used for temporary image hosting required by the Instagram API. Images are not stored beyond what is necessary for posting. See Cloudinary's Privacy Policy.
  • Wikipedia — publicly available articles are used as sources when we prepare factual posts.

Pinterest integration

Our posting app ("MoonThoughtsHub", Luminary Media Group LLC) connects to a single Pinterest account: our own business account, @MoonThoughtsHub. It is not offered to other Pinterest users.

  • Sign-in: access is granted through Pinterest's own OAuth permission page. The app never sees or stores a Pinterest password or session cookie.
  • What the app reads: our account's username and business name (to confirm the right account is connected) and the names of our own boards (to choose where a Pin goes).
  • What the app writes: Pins of our own images, with our own text, on our own boards; and, if one is missing, a board of our own.
  • What is stored: only the access and refresh tokens Pinterest issues, kept in a private file on our own computer. They are never shared, published or sent anywhere except back to Pinterest.
  • No other users' data: the app does not read, collect or store other Pinterest users' profiles, Pins, boards or followers.
  • Revoking access: the connection can be removed at any time in Pinterest's settings (Security and logins → Apps), after which the stored tokens no longer work.

How we use information

We do not sell, rent, or share any personal data with third parties for marketing purposes. The third-party services listed above are used solely to operate our automated content publishing pipeline.

Content generation

All content posted by MoonThoughts Hub is prepared locally on our own hardware: text with a local AI language model (Ollama), backgrounds and music with local models, and every post is checked before it is published. Post content is not sent to external AI services or cloud-based model providers. The only external data transmissions are the finished posts sent to the platforms listed above.

Data retention

This website does not store visitor data. Published social media content is retained on the respective platforms subject to their own retention policies.

Your rights

If you reside in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, or California, you may have rights regarding personal data under GDPR, UK GDPR, or CCPA respectively. As we do not collect personal data from website visitors, these rights have limited practical application here. For interactions with our social media accounts, please refer to the relevant platform's privacy settings.

Children's privacy

Our content is intended for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us and we will take steps to remove it.

External links

This website contains links to external sites (Instagram and others). We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those sites. We encourage you to read their privacy policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of this website after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
